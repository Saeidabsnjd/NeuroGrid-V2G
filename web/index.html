Game Theory Insights

Nash Equilibrium (NE)

Approximate state reached by our decentralized, selfish agents learning to maximize their own battery utilities under dynamic network pricing.

Social Optimum (SO)

The global system-minimum cost scenario calculated by our centralized mathematical solver where a central entity plans all charging.

Price of Anarchy (PoA)

PoA = Cost(NE) / Cost(SO)

Measures efficiency loss from decentralized actions. A PoA of 1.0 represents perfect market efficiency.
